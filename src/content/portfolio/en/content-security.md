Owned content-protection products at PallyCon (DoveRunner) — a cloud SaaS protecting premium OTT and streaming content for media & entertainment customers worldwide.

Two complementary lines:

- **Multi-DRM** — defined and managed the multi-DRM SDK and cloud service across Widevine, PlayReady, and FairPlay: packaging and license delivery for secure multi-screen playback.
- **Forensic & distributor watermarking** — session-based watermarking with anti-piracy monitoring to trace and deter leaks of premium content.

The work spanned the full product arc: OTT/streaming and DRM market research, roadmap and requirements, and technical pre-sales and support for customers and partners worldwide.
