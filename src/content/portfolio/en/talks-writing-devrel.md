Years of developer-facing writing and documentation in media-tech, increasingly overlapping with AI for knowledge work.

- **Technical blog** — articles on multi-DRM, forensic watermarking, OTT/streaming, anti-piracy, and DRM on AWS Media Services, plus recent pieces on using Claude and Jira MCP to speed up product work. Published on Medium in English and Korean.
- **Developer documentation** — built and maintain the DoveRunner developer docs site on Astro + Starlight, covering content security (multi-DRM, watermarking, anti-piracy) and mobile app security SDKs.

The documentation work is where agent-readiness gets concrete: structuring docs that both engineers and AI agents can reliably retrieve and act on.
