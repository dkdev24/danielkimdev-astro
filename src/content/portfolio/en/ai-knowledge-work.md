The lead thread of my current work: how AI changes the way we capture knowledge,
automate busywork, and write documentation that agents — not just people — can use.

A concrete example: I used Claude with the Jira MCP to onboard faster as a new product
owner, turning scattered tickets and project context into knowledge I could actually act
on — written up on Medium. The same thread runs through my agent-ready documentation work
and the LLM-friendly digital garden that powers this site.
