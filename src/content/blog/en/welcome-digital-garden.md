This is a digital garden, not a blog in the tidy, finished sense. Some notes are
seedlings; some are essays I've tended for a while. They grow in public.

The throughline is **AI for knowledge work**: how AI reshapes the way we manage
knowledge, automate the busywork, and write documentation that machines can use.
My years in media-tech product and developer relations are the proof, not a second
topic — they keep the AI takes specific.

Pull up a chair. Things will change.
